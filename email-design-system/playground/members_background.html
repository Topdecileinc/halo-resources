Members background

600×600 square card with a background photo, headline, body text, and a CTA button overlaid in the upper-left. Downloaded from Figma (node 4645:425) and built to the email rules. Text is overlaid via a background image with an Outlook fallback color. Edit the image URL to preview a real photo. Note: the right padding (200px) on the body text keeps it in the left portion of the card so the photo is visible on the right. The headline uses 36px (Figma spec; style-guide H1 is 34px).
